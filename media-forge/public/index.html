MEDIA FORGE by ShadyCraftBuilt for the things you make.

A format for almost every idea.

Media Forge converts files on your device. Its single-thread FFmpeg engine loads from a version-pinned CDN on the first video, audio or advanced image conversion. No media bytes are uploaded.

Video MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, AVI

Animation Video → GIF, with trim, speed, frame rate, loop and palette controls

Audio MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AAC — including audio extraction from video

Images PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, TIFF

Still frames Video → PNG, JPEG, WebP at a selected timestamp

PDF editing

Edit PDF provides a page preview for adding text, images, freehand drawings and a handwritten signature. Existing text is not edited. Added elements are flattened into transparent image overlays; original page content remains. A handwritten signature is not a cryptographic digital signature.

Merge files in queue order; split, extract, remove or reorder pages; rotate, number or watermark pages; crop visible page bounds; flatten form fields; optimize PDF structure; or compress image-heavy PDFs by rasterizing their pages. Crop does not redact hidden content. Raster compression loses selectable text, annotations, forms, links and signatures and can increase file size. Structural optimization does not guarantee a smaller file. Digital signatures are not preserved by editing.

Documents, PDF & archives

Document conversions extract text and rebuild a simple document. They do not preserve original layouts or images. PDF to PowerPoint preserves each page as an image, not editable slide elements. PDF to Excel/CSV extracts page numbers and text lines, not reconstructed tables. SVG output embeds a rasterized page. Scanned PDFs need OCR, which is not included.

Document inputs are limited to 25 MB. ZIP containers are limited to 500 entries and 64 MB expanded. Spreadsheets support XLSX, CSV, TSV and row-array JSON; the first or named worksheet is exported, using cached formula results. PDF page conversion supports up to 50 selected pages. ZIP and uncompressed TAR can be repacked; RAR/7Z are not supported.

Good to know

  • 150 MB per file, 300 MB per batch, up to 12 files. Mobile devices may run out of memory earlier. Browser conversions are slower than desktop converters.
  • Input codec support varies. Corrupted, encrypted, DRM-protected or unsupported media will report an error. File extensions alone do not guarantee compatibility.
  • Animated images become a single still image in Image converter. Use Video to GIF for animated output. JPEG and BMP do not preserve transparency. Browser JPEG encoding uses a white background.
  • For GIFs, duration is 0.1–30 seconds and width is at most 1280 pixels. Other timed conversions: duration 0 means the remainder of the file. Playback speed changes output duration.
  • Image quality affects JPEG and WebP. PNG, BMP and TIFF are lossless. Dimensions preserve aspect ratio; width and height together define a bounding box.
  • Converting to a lossless format cannot recover details lost in the source. A container change may re-encode media. Video output preserves one primary video and audio stream; subtitles and attachments are not carried over.
  • Cancel stops the current conversion. Completed files remain downloadable until removed, cleared or the page is closed. Refreshing clears the queue.
  • Use a recent desktop Chrome, Edge or Firefox browser. Downloaded formats may require a compatible player even when conversion succeeds.
